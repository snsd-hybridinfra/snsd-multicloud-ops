# Risk Register

Track risks that may affect scenario quality, evidence integrity, cost, safety, or repository consistency.

| Risk ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Scope expansion | Repository may drift beyond locked validation scope. | Require scope review and ADR before expanding technologies or implementation boundaries. | Open |
| R002 | Cloud cost increase | Future validation could create billable resources unexpectedly. | Require explicit approval, cost guardrails, and teardown evidence before real cloud execution. | Open |
| R003 | Evidence missing | Scenarios may be difficult to review or reproduce. | Require evidence status updates and validation table completion for every scenario. | Open |
| R004 | Secret leakage | Sensitive material could be committed to the repository. | Enforce `.gitignore`, evidence sanitization, and review checklist before commits. | Open |
| R005 | OneDrive sync conflict | Local sync behavior may create conflicts or partial file updates. | Check working tree status before and after large edits; avoid simultaneous edits. | Open |
| R006 | Codex modifying unrelated files | Automation may change files outside the requested scope. | Require scope confirmation and final status review for every task. | Open |
| R007 | Scenario implementation inconsistency | Scenarios may use different structures or criteria. | Use `docs/scenario-template.md` and update tracking matrices consistently. | Open |
| R008 | Non-executed artifacts mistaken for runtime evidence | Static, sample, synthetic, or pasted content may create false implementation or validation claims. | Keep all scenarios NOT_STARTED and evidence NOT_READY until authorized real execution; quarantine provenance-uncertain artifacts and review evidence before status advancement. | Open |
| R009 | Host resource exhaustion | Running OpenStack and the complete local-service stack together can exceed effective memory and pressure SSD capacity. | Enforce Profiles A-D, preserve approximately 8GB host reserve, limit Nova to one small instance initially, and keep snapshots short-lived. | Open |
| R010 | Single-node OpenStack control and compute failure domain | One AIO VM outage affects APIs, networking, scheduling, and compute; HA is not provided. | Keep the environment lab-only, document the limitation, and do not make production resilience claims. | Open |
| R011 | Lab Kolla image and nested-virtualization dependency | Pre-built test images and VMware nested virtualization may differ from supported production deployment behavior. | Treat results as portfolio-lab evidence only; record versions and troubleshooting without proprietary images or acquisition details. | Open |
| R012 | OpenStack provider and storage limitations | The provider path depends on one NIC; Cinder/Ceph and backup/restore are not implemented. | Limit workload to one small Nova instance and require package-owned storage/recovery evidence before broader claims. | Open |
| R013 | Runtime evidence authority ambiguity | Repository readers may confuse operator-executed deployment/data-plane evidence with later restricted Codex execution. | Label both authorities separately; Codex-executed claims are limited to the forced read-only `validate-all` result and boundary tests. | Mitigated |
| R014 | Zero Trust maturity overclaim | Design or partial lab evidence may be presented as a source maturity level. | Require capability-specific source-table checks, bounded scope, evidence references, confidence, and exceptions. | Open |
| R015 | Unsupported capability mapping | Broad infrastructure work may be mapped to unrelated identity, endpoint, application, or data controls. | Permit `NOT_MAPPED` and `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; review mappings against the actual package scope. | Open |
| R016 | Source terminology drift | Unofficial translations or renamed controls may break traceability to the Korean source. | Preserve canonical Korean display names and source numbering; keep English slugs secondary and stable. | Open |
| R017 | Partial lab represented as enterprise implementation | Bounded package results may be generalized beyond the disposable lab. | State environment, authority, path, evidence level, and assessment boundary on every claim. | Open |
| R018 | Planned technology marked implemented | Planned package work may be mistaken for deployed controls. | Keep package metadata authoritative and separate mapping, implementation, validation, acceptance, evidence, and maturity fields. | Open |
| R019 | Cross-cutting capability omission | Visibility/analytics and automation/integration may be omitted from pillar-focused work. | Include both cross-cutting domains in taxonomy, matrices, gaps, roadmap, and assessment review. | Open |
| R020 | Backlog target mistaken for current maturity | Readers may treat a proposed lab target as achieved maturity. | Keep current and target fields separate; require Gate ZT-4 evidence acceptance before reassessment. | Open |
| R021 | Dependency or wave drift | A capability may be scheduled before its technical prerequisites. | Validate capability IDs, cycles, wave membership, and later-wave dependency violations in the Zero Trust validator. | Open |
| R022 | Control-pattern technology lock-in | Candidate products may be mistaken for approved architecture or operating controls. | Label every unproven technology CANDIDATE ONLY and require ADR/scope approval before adoption. | Open |
| R023 | EVE restricted validation endpoint unavailable | The endpoint was missing during the first gate check. | Installed and verified the dedicated account, forced dispatcher, exact sudoers rule, read-only validator, and boundary denials while preserving operator access. | Mitigated |
| R024 | EVE validation alias remains privileged | The alias initially used an operator account and lab key. | Replaced only the validator alias after direct dedicated-key and boundary validation; preserved the prior config backup and operator access. | Mitigated |
| R025 | OpenStack validator summary format drift | The installed validator previously omitted the package-required final WARN count. | Backed up the installed script, deployed the reviewed rendered template through the dedicated operator channel, and repeated allowed and boundary validation. | Mitigated |
| R026 | No persistent router interface ACL binding | The prior absence is closed for one bounded directional policy: the DMZ ACL is bound in running/startup configuration and passed allow, deny, counter, fixed-path, and rollback checks. Broader micro-segmentation remains outside scope. | Preserve the accepted ACL and restricted endpoint; require separate design, rollback, and evidence for any policy expansion. | Mitigated |
| R027 | Telemetry has no accepted persistent central storage | The prior storage gap is closed for approved sanitized JSONL on one monitoring VM; pinned Grafana/Loki/Alloy passed health, retention, query, restart, and secret-boundary checks. Broader sources and HA remain absent. | Preserve single-node sanitized-input scope; require separate approval for new sources, external alerting, identity integration, or HA. | Mitigated |
| R028 | Target architecture mistaken for implementation or maturity | ZT-ARC-001 selects Advanced targets and uses the local OPTIMAL_READY designation, which could be misread as deployed controls or an official maturity result. | Keep the package DESIGN_ONLY, current maturity UNASSESSED, capability-specific acceptance mandatory, and OPTIMAL_READY explicitly non-official. | Open |
| R029 | Architecture work collides with active monitoring implementation | Concurrent edits could overwrite ignored ZT-VIS-002 scripts, raw output, or future Compose/configuration work. | Treat `.runtime/zero-trust/zt-vis-002/` and active monitoring configuration as protected; architecture work may inspect but not modify, restart, or accept it. | Open |
| R030 | Identity package ID ambiguity | The approved queue uses ZT-ID-001 while the target roadmap includes ZT-ID-002 as a later candidate. | Preserve both meanings, keep ZT-ID-001 as the approved predecessor, and require an ADR/package record before changing identifiers or scope. | Open |
| R031 | Monitoring VM reboot-required state | The live endpoint pilot reports a pending reboot marker; immediate reboot could interrupt the accepted telemetry stack. | Keep service health validated, prohibit automatic reboot, and schedule an operator-reviewed maintenance window with pre/post telemetry checks. | Open |
| R032 | Dedicated endpoint vulnerability scanner absent | Native security-update state does not establish complete vulnerability coverage. | Keep the result `ASSESSMENT_LIMITED`; evaluate one bounded candidate with resource, privacy, compatibility, and rollback review before any installation. | Open |
| R033 | Partial application SBOM coverage | The Compose-derived CycloneDX record contains only three direct images and no transitive packages. | Keep `sbom_status: PARTIAL`; require a reviewed dedicated scanner before any complete-component or vulnerability-coverage claim. | Open |
| R034 | Mutable application image references | The bounded pilot uses pinned tags without verified immutable digests or provenance. | Keep digest and provenance gates partial; require reviewed digest capture and verification before deployment authority expands. | Open |
| R035 | Alloy container root UID | Alloy runs as container UID 0 even though capabilities are dropped, no-new-privileges is set, and the root filesystem is read-only. | Preserve compensating controls and test non-root compatibility with rollback before changing the running workload. | Open |
| R036 | Application security scanner absent | Built-in dependency-free scans cover controlled secrets and Compose policy but do not establish CVE coverage, artifact signing, or registry trust. | Keep dedicated scan, signature, and vulnerability states false; evaluate tools only through a separate compatibility and approval gate. | Open |
| R037 | Platform backup and restore unknown | Existing monitoring and OpenStack evidence does not prove backup creation, integrity, or restoration. | Keep both records `UNKNOWN`; require separate live-source ownership, encryption, capacity, rollback, and isolated-restore approval. | Open |
| R038 | Synthetic pilot backup unencrypted | The validated backup contains non-sensitive generated data and uses a local copy because no approved encryption tool or key boundary exists. | Limit acceptance to integrity/isolation; introduce encryption only with a reviewed key lifecycle and rollback plan. | Open |
| R039 | Detection-only DLP boundary | Redacted repository scanning detects controlled patterns but does not prevent exfiltration or establish complete DLP. | Keep blocking/quarantine/delete/modify/rotate actions prohibited until separately approved enforcement and recovery tests exist. | Open |
| R040 | Manual bounded classification | Seven known assets are manually labeled, so unobserved or newly added data may remain outside the inventory. | Keep discovery claims partial; validate references and require owner review before adding a source or automatic classifier. | Open |
| R041 | Data encryption coverage unknown | Workstation, monitoring volume, and OpenStack storage/API encryption are not evidenced; loopback telemetry and the synthetic backup are not encrypted. | Preserve `UNKNOWN`/`NOT_ENCRYPTED` truth states and require direct configuration/runtime evidence before promotion. | Open |
| R042 | OpenStack current runtime degradation | APIs and containers are available, but two console markers and two test-instance reachability checks fail at the current 46 PASS/0 WARN/4 FAIL baseline. | Keep `CURRENT_DEGRADED`; accept only the exact known result in SYS orchestration and require a separately approved diagnosis/remediation run with rollback before changing the target. | Open |
| R043 | Continuous system integrity monitoring absent | Five safe repository configuration hashes are point-in-time matches; sensitive endpoint and generated Kolla configuration are intentionally not hashed. | Keep continuous FIM false, preserve secret-content exclusions, and require a reviewed host-local integrity design before adding collection or enforcement. | Open |
| R044 | System recovery readiness incomplete | Configuration and rollback references exist, but no full OpenStack, EVE, router, monitoring-host, or workstation restore is validated. | Keep full-system restore count zero and require owner, capacity, encryption, isolated target, rollback, and service-impact approval for each future recovery test. | Open |
| R045 | Mixed platform maintenance gaps | The monitoring VM requires reviewed reboot maintenance, lacks a dedicated scanner, and the virtual router uses a legacy lab image. | Prohibit automatic reboot, upgrade, scanner installation, or image replacement; use separate compatibility, maintenance, rollback, and post-change validation gates. | Open |
| R046 | Automation scope expansion | A fixed validation orchestrator could be mistaken for authority to run arbitrary commands or future R4-R8 changes. | Keep handler registration in reviewed code, default deny, positive parameter allowlists, immutable plan hashes, and non-executable R4-R8 references; require separate change approval for every new handler or live target. | Open |
| R047 | Single-workstation execution coordination | Local lock files prevent duplicate package workflows only on one workstation and cannot coordinate distributed runners. | Retain owner metadata, bounded stale-lock reporting, and no silent deletion; prohibit multi-runner or scheduled use until a separately validated coordination design exists. | Open |
| R048 | Partial integrated automation result | The live workflow preserves predecessor warnings, including OpenStack `CURRENT_DEGRADED`, two unassessed configuration records, monitoring maintenance, and scanner gaps. | Keep the workflow `PARTIAL`; never downgrade mandatory failures; use ZT-CV-001 to classify acceptance and regression before selecting an RV target. | Open |
| R049 | Notification and incident handoff absent | No reviewed email, Slack, webhook, ticketing, GitHub issue, or incident-management integration exists. | Generate sanitized local proposals only; require channel ownership, data minimization, secret handling, failure, rollback, and operator review before any external delivery. | Open |
| R050 | Time-separated repeatability and scheduled evidence absent | ZT-CV-001 has one accepted EC3 cycle and ZT-RV-001 has zero accepted campaign runs; immediate retries could be mistaken for independence and a prepared schedule could be mistaken for EC5. | Enforce the immutable not-before timestamp and PT24H separation, require three unique consecutive accepted runs, keep history append explicit, and block all ZT-SCH-001 preparation until EC4. | Open |
