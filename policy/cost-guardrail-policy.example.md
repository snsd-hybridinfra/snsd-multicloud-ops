# Cost Guardrail Policy — Non-Production Example

Cost-impacting changes require estimated monthly cost evidence and Incremental cost delta classification. Critical cost increases require approval. Temporary exceptions require owner, reason, expiry, approval, rollback condition, and evidence reference. Orphans map to S046. Evidence must not contain real billing data; real invoices, billing exports, and cloud bills must not be committed. External cost tools are optional, not required. S050 owns final reporting.
