# ML Anomaly Detection Policy — Non-Production Example

Anomaly detection must use metric-only S047 evidence, a documented threshold profile, and output anomaly score, anomaly decision, and evidence reference. Human review is required before operational action. SIEM, Wazuh, EDR, raw logs, packet captures/payloads, malware detection, threat hunting, LLM-based analysis, and automated blocking are prohibited. S049 owns human-readable reports and S050 owns final reporting.
