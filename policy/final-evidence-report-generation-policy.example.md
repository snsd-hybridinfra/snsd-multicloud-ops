# Final Evidence Report Generation Policy — Non-Production Example

The final report accounts for all 50 scenarios and includes scenario/evidence status, validation commands, risk register, excluded scope, and safety boundary summaries. It must contain no secrets, real infrastructure identifiers, production logs/metrics/billing data, SIEM/Wazuh/EDR telemetry, or packet payloads. External audit certification and production compliance approval claims are prohibited. PDF/PPTX export is out of scope unless separately requested.
