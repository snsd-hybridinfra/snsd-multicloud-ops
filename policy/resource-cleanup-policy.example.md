# Resource Cleanup Policy — Non-Production Example

Candidates require owner evidence, retention classification, cost classification, dependency risk review, and rollback or recovery note. Critical cleanup requires approval. Active exceptions require owner, reason, expiry, approval, rollback condition, and evidence reference. Real resource deletion must not be executed by validation scripts. Terraform destroy and cloud delete commands are OUT OF SCOPE. Cost-bearing cleanup maps to retired-numbered-case and final reporting to retired-numbered-case.
