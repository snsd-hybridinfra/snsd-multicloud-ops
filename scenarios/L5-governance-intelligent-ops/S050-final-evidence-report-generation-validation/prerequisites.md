# Prerequisites

- `docs/scope-lock.md` and `docs/excluded-scope.md` have been reviewed.
- S001 through S049 scenario and evidence directories exist.
- Required tracking and inspection documents exist.
- Scenario status and evidence status values follow their documented models.
- Scenario validation remains authoritative; S050 only aggregates its results.
- Report placeholders such as `<scenario-id>`, `<evidence-status>`, `<validation-result>`, and `<coverage-rate>` are used.
- No credentials, secrets, private keys, tfstate, kubeconfig, cloud identifiers, billing identifiers, or account-specific values are available to the report.
- Reviewers understand that the planned report is not a formal audit or compliance certificate.
