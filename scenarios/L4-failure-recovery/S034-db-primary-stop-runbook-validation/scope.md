# Scope

## Included

- Static runbook, commands, thirteen-phase criteria, debug-only Ansible, symbolic metrics, and ten samples.
- Primary role, replication baseline, manual stop, outage/write impact, no-promotion Replica state, manual recovery, restored role/replication/catch-up.
- Dump, payload, row, credential, connection-string, address, key, secret, and no-execution safety.

## Excluded

- Database/monitoring access, SQL, service stop/start, Ansible execution, promotion, failover, replication changes, automatic recovery, production-grade HA, or cross-cloud DR.
- Real hosts/IPs, credentials, data, GTIDs, binlogs, dumps, payloads, or secrets.
